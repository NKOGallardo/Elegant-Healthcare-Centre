What We Offer

Our Healthcare Services

Comprehensive medical care and premium wellness treatments, expertly delivered to restore, revitalise, and support every stage of your health journey.

Vitamin Drip (Cocktail)
A powerful blend of essential vitamins delivered directly into the bloodstream for maximum absorption and rapid results.
Jet Fuel
Vitamin C, Vitamin B Complex & Collagen — premium formula for energy, skin health, and total revitalisation.
Neurobion (Vitamin C)
Targeted Vitamin C infusion to support nerve health, immunity, and overall wellbeing with lasting effects.
Glow Drip
Skin-brightening formula enriched with glutathione and antioxidants for a luminous, radiant complexion.
Immune Booster Drip
Fortify your immune system with a high-dose vitamin and mineral infusion designed to keep illness at bay.
Biotin / Vitamin B7
Essential for healthy hair, nails, and skin metabolism. Promotes growth and strengthens from within.
Weight Loss Drip
Metabolic-boosting infusion to support your weight management journey through enhanced fat burning and energy.
Sports Endurance & Recovery
Optimise athletic performance and accelerate muscle recovery with this professional-grade hydration formula.
Energy Booster Drip
Combat fatigue and restore vitality with our targeted energy drip, packed with B vitamins and essential cofactors.
Hangover Booster
Fast-acting rehydration and detox drip to restore balance and get you back on your feet quickly.
All Family Planning Services
Comprehensive contraception counselling and planning to support informed reproductive choices tailored to your needs.
IUD Insertion & Removal
Safe and professional intrauterine device insertion and removal performed in a sterile, clinical environment.
Implanon Insertion & Removal
Subdermal contraceptive implant placement and removal carried out with precision by qualified healthcare professionals.
Morning After Pill
Emergency contraception with professional counselling, available promptly when timing matters most.
Pregnancy Testing
Accurate and confidential pregnancy testing with compassionate professional support and guidance on next steps.
Pap Smear (Cervical Screening)
Cervical cancer screening in a private, respectful setting. Early detection saves lives — book your pap smear today.
ANC First Visit & Follow-up
Antenatal care from your first visit through ongoing check-ups, monitoring both mother and baby throughout pregnancy.
Birth Options
Flexible birth planning — choose between a comfortable centre delivery or a safe, assisted home birth with our qualified midwives.
3-Day Postnatal Care
Dedicated postnatal care for both mother and baby over the critical first three days — monitoring, support, and education.
Baby Wellness & Immunizations
Regular baby wellness checks and scheduled immunizations to monitor growth, development, and build immunity from day one.
General Consultation
Thorough general medical consultation with the option to include or exclude medication — flexible, patient-centred care.
Follow-up Consultations
Continuity of care with scheduled follow-up visits to track your progress, adjust treatment, and ensure full recovery.
STI Treatment
Confidential sexually transmitted infection diagnosis and treatment with medication included — private, non-judgmental care.
HIV Counselling, Testing & Management
Compassionate HIV counselling, accurate testing, and ongoing management plans to support long-term health and wellbeing.
Blood Pressure Monitoring
Regular blood pressure checks to detect hypertension early and manage cardiovascular health proactively.
Glucose (Blood Sugar) Testing
Fast and accurate blood glucose testing to screen for diabetes and monitor sugar levels in existing patients.
Cholesterol Testing
Lipid profile testing to assess cholesterol levels and reduce your risk of heart disease and stroke.
DNA Testing
DNA testing facilitated with professional guidance. Note: laboratory fees are paid separately by the patient.
Initial Wound Dressing
Professional first-line wound assessment and dressing with sterile technique to promote healing and prevent infection.
Wound Care Follow-up
Ongoing wound management and re-dressing sessions to monitor healing progress and adjust care as needed.
Suture (Stitch) Removal
Safe and hygienic suture removal once healing is confirmed, performed with care to minimise discomfort and scarring.
